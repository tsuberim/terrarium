---
title: Energy & glims
description: Deflationary economy and free-mint budget.
---

Energy is the sole in-world resource. **Glims** (◆) are the display unit: 1 glim = 100,000 energy units.

## Flow

| Class | Examples |
|-------|----------|
| **Import** | Deploy (credits → creature), dev faucet |
| **Transfer** | Eat corpse, spawn, suicide payout |
| **Destroy** | Opcode gas, action costs, death leak |
| **Free mint** | Terrain food (budgeted) |

**Budget rule:** for every 2 energy destroyed, at most 1 free unit may be minted over the world's lifetime.

Deploy costs **corpse base + extra glims** you choose. Suicide returns remaining energy to your credits.

See [internal/engineering/sim/energy-budget.md](https://github.com/tsuberim/terrarium/blob/main/docs/internal/engineering/sim/energy-budget.md) for full ledger details.

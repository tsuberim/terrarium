Look down. Write code. Stay alive.

Terrarium is a 2D realtime MMO programming game. If your creature dies, you are gone. There is no click-to-play agency — only programs and physics.

Kernel and skin

The kernel runs the world: deterministic physics, mass accounting, and a tiny guest instruction set. It is auth-free and conservation-first.

The skin is a retro camera. It does not run the economy or mint mass. You look through it; you do not play through it.

Seven verbs

  • thrust — move; costs mass
  • sense — look; costs mass
  • absorb — take inert mass (explicit, not auto-eat)
  • dump — leave inert matter in the world
  • attach / split — bodies (later milestones)
  • cash out — mass leaves the box as money (later)

Sleep is free. Idle life is cheap. Thought and motion are not.

Open torus

The world wraps on both axes — move off an edge and re-enter on the opposite side. No petri-dish rim. Large enough to feel like a map.

Platform

Around the game: Firebase Auth for humans, a credits ledger, scoped API tokens, and POST /v1/spawn to cash credits into mass at a position. An always-on native host runs the authoritative world; the API owns billing.

Use the console for balance and tokens. Use play to watch the sim.
